# Product Guidelines

## Copywriting & Tone
- **Empathetic & Supportive:** The application deals with health and potentially stressful situations. All user-facing copy must be empathetic, reassuring, and friendly. Avoid cold, overly clinical language, while ensuring instructions remain clear and unambiguous.

## Design Paradigm
- **Utility-First:** The visual design must prioritize utility. Use clear typography and strict state-based color coding (e.g., Green for Monitor, Amber for Home Care, Red for Urgent) to convey critical triage information at a glance. Embellishments should not detract from the core clinical information.

## Accessibility Priorities
- **High Contrast:** All text and critical UI elements must strictly comply with WCAG AA contrast ratios, ensuring legibility for users with varying vision capabilities.
- **Ease of Interaction:** Ensure all interactive elements (buttons, toggles, icon grids) have a minimum touch target size of 44x44pt, minimizing mis-taps.

## Offline & State Feedback
- **Inline Indicators:** Use subtle inline indicators (such as icons next to specific actions) to communicate offline status, sync events, or pending operations, rather than intrusive blocking modals or large banners.
